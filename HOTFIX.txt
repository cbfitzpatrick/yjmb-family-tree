YJMB v17.4 — lossless protected tree synchronization hotfix

Fixes the case where a person/relationship exists in the protected exported XLSX
but disappears from the encrypted website/admin spreadsheet during generation.

- Website generation no longer omits workbook people because RAT Year is unresolved.
- Raw VET/RAT/source fields are never blanked merely because a visual edge is ambiguous.
- CI rebuilds use a fresh resolution cache, so stale cached skips cannot hide corrected relationships.
- A workbook↔encrypted-site parity check fails closed if a named workbook row or source field is lost.
- Relationship claims can still identify a unique person even when a real visual-parent conflict omits the edge.
- Manual workflow_dispatch now forces a rebuild/deploy from secure/master_workbook.enc even when the auto queue is empty.
- Connectors are restored to the historical gray #777777 single stroke with no white outline.
- Includes the v17.3 idempotent synchronization/correction fixes.

No Worker changes and no new secrets are required.
